# Brief 07: Marketing and Content Agents

## Mission
Design agent workflows for **marketing**: positioning, content, SEO, social, email, launch, and analytics. Human brand control is required.

## Key questions
1. Which marketing tasks do agents reliably do well in 2026, and which still need a human? Cite evidence where possible.
2. Content pipeline: brief → draft → brand and fact check → approval → schedule → publish → measure. Which tools and APIs support each step? Gamma and Google Workspace are available.
3. SEO and GEO (optimization for AI answer engines): what are current practices, and which tools are agent-friendly?
4. Automation platforms that agents can drive: compare n8n, Make, and Zapier, plus MCP servers for social, email, and analytics.
5. Analytics feedback loops: product analytics and attribution that agents can query to decide what to make next.
6. Brand voice: how should it be encoded (style guides as skills, eval sets for tone)?
7. Compliance: disclosure rules for AI-generated content, platform ToS on automation, and GDPR for email.

## Seed candidates (verify)
n8n, Typefully, Buffer and Postiz, Resend, Loops, PostHog, Plausible, Google Search Console API, Ahrefs and Semrush APIs, Clay, and Gamma.

## Special deliverable
A **weekly marketing operating loop** (agents plus human gates) and a **launch checklist workflow**.
